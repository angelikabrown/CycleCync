2. The Problem
Questions to answer:
What frustrations do women experience that inspired this app?
Things that occur naturally each cycle are treated wrongly like symptoms of a sickness or a medical problem. When it is just a natural occurrence.
Not knowing the signs of when their period happens, periods “happen” to women not something that is a natural part of a cycle.
PMS does not just come on randomly. There is a reason. It can be managed once you get a feel of your natural rhythm.
Each cycle is unique and can’t be treated like one size fits all. What happens for one woman may not be another woman’s issue. Not all cycles have the same ups and downs.
Why aren't existing cycle trackers solving this problem?
Cycle trackers main purpose is increasing chances of pregnancy in couples who want to get pregnant. But teenagers have cycles. Pre-menopausal women have cycles. Single women have cycles. Not everyone who needs a cycle tracker needs one to get pregnant. So there is a gap there.
Why is tracking alone not enough?
I don’t understand this question
Why do people stop using cycle tracking apps?
When they get pregnant.
