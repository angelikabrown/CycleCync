CycleCync MVP - Epics and User Stories


Epic 1 – Account
Story 1: As a new user, I want to create an account so that my cycle history is securely saved over time. ⭐⭐⭐
Story 2: As a returning user, I want to log in so I can continue tracking my progress. ⭐⭐⭐

Epic 2 – Daily Check-In
Story 1: As a user, I want to record my basal body temperature so the app can recognize temperature patterns over multiple cycles. ⭐⭐⭐
Story 2: As a user, I want to record my mood so I can better understand how it changes throughout my cycle. ⭐⭐⭐
Story 3: As a user, I want to record my energy level so I can identify periods when I naturally feel more or less energetic. ⭐⭐⭐
Story 4: As a user, I want to record how well I slept so I can see whether my sleep changes during different phases of my cycle. ⭐⭐
Story 5: As a user, I want to edit previous entries so I can correct mistakes if I entered something incorrectly. ⭐⭐⭐

Epic 3 – Dashboard
Story 1: As a user, I want to view my temperature graph so I can recognize changes across my cycle. ⭐⭐⭐
Story 2: As a user, I want to compare previous cycles so I can identify recurring patterns. ⭐⭐
Story 3: As a user, I want to view my cycle on a calendar so I can quickly review past entries. ⭐⭐⭐
Story 4: As a user, I want to see what the app has learned about me so I can better understand my own patterns. ⭐⭐⭐

Epic 4 – AI Coach
Story 1: As a user, I want to ask questions in plain language so I don't have to interpret charts myself. ⭐⭐⭐
Story 2: As a user, I want recommendations based on my own historical data so the advice feels personal rather than generic. ⭐⭐⭐
Story 3: As a user, I want the AI to explain why it made a recommendation so I can build trust in the insights. ⭐⭐

Epic 5 – Learning
Story 1: As a new user, I want to learn one small concept each day so I understand why I'm collecting this information. ⭐⭐⭐
Story 2: As a new user, I want encouragement during my first three cycles so I stay motivated while the app is learning about me. ⭐⭐⭐
Story 3: As a user, I want educational content to gradually transition into personalized insights as more of my data becomes available. ⭐⭐⭐

Epic 6 – 90-Day Discovery Challenge
Story 1: As a new user, I want to join a 90-Day Cycle Discovery Challenge so I stay committed long enough to uncover meaningful patterns. ⭐⭐
Story 2: As a user, I want to track my progress through the challenge so I know how close I am to receiving personalized insights. ⭐⭐
Story 3: As a user, I want milestones along the way so I feel encouraged to continue tracking consistently. ⭐⭐
